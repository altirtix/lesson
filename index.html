Our solar system is full of remarkable places beyond the eight planets. Follow the clues left by ancient worlds and discover what makes our cosmic neighborhood so extraordinary.

See the quick facts ↓

From Earth's familiar Moon to hidden oceans beneath the ice of Europa and Enceladus, these worlds may hold some of the solar system's biggest mysteries.

Rocky asteroids and icy comets are ancient leftovers from the solar system's formation—time capsules that help tell our origin story.

Past Neptune lies the Kuiper Belt, home to dwarf planets like Pluto and countless small icy bodies orbiting at the edge of our planetary neighborhood.
